What is EBillZen?

EBillZen is a specialized home automation tool designed by Innovate Nest Labs. It specifically targets the pain points of property management in Bangladesh, making utility billing simple and error-free.

Goodbye Khata, Hello System
No more manual calculations on paper. EBillZen automates the entire process, ensuring every unit is accounted for accurately.
