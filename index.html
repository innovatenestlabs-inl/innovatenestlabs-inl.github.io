What is EBillZen?

EBillZen is a specialized home automation tool designed by Innovate Nest Labs. It specifically targets the pain points of property management in Bangladesh.

Goodbye Khata, Hello System:
No more manual calculations on paper or messy "Khata" records. EBillZen automates the billing process for home owners, market owners, and shop complexes.

Accurate Calculation

Input meter details once, and then simply enter the current reading. The system calculates everything instantly with zero errors.

Transparency

Generate digital bill summaries and share them instantly with tenants via WhatsApp or SMS. Build trust through clear data.

Versatile Use

Perfect for residential sub-meters, commercial shop units, or large market complexes where multiple meters need tracking.
