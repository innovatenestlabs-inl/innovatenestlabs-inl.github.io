Privacy Policy

How we handle your data at EBillZen

Last updated: Feb 02, 2026

Overview: This Privacy Policy explains how EBillZen (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the EBillZen mobile application (the “App”).

EBillZen helps homeowners manage sub-meter information and calculate monthly bills for renters/tenants. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect
  • Account & Authentication Data: Phone number (for phone login), and basic Google account information (for Google sign-in) such as your name, email address, and profile image.
  • Meter & Renter Information: Meter name/identifier, meter readings, unit/rate details, renter/tenant name, contact details, and stored notes.
  • Billing & Calculation Data: Monthly bill calculations, previous/current readings, and calculated bill summaries.
  • Device/App Data: Basic technical data such as device type, OS version, and app version for security and troubleshooting purposes.
Note: We do not require or collect GPS/location data.
2. How We Use Your Information
  • To create and manage accounts via phone and Google.
  • To save and display meter and renter details.
  • To calculate monthly bills and provide dashboard summaries.
  • To provide support, improve reliability, and prevent fraud.
3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored securely on cloud servers and databases. While we use administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data, absolute security over the internet cannot be guaranteed. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own authentication methods (phone access and Google account).

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Sharing occurs only with:

  • Service Providers: Trusted vendors for hosting, database management, and authentication.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, or government request.
  • Safety & Rights: To protect users or the security of the App.
5. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services for essential operations (e.g., Google Sign-In, phone OTP/SMS, cloud hosting). External sharing of bill details via WhatsApp or SMS is governed by the specific privacy policies of those external applications.

6. Your Choices & Controls
  • Update Information: Modify meter and renter data directly within the app.
  • Delete Meter Data: Remove specific meters and history from your account.
  • Account Deletion: Users can request full account deletion via the in-app option. Once deleted, all data removal is irreversible.
