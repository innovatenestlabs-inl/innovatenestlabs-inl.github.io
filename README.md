**EBillZen** is a specialized property management and home automation tool designed by **Innovate Nest Labs**. It empowers homeowners, shop owners, and market managers in Bangladesh to move beyond the traditional "Khata" (manual ledger) and embrace digital automation.

---

## 📖 Overview

In many residential and commercial complexes, managing electricity sub-meters is a manual, error-prone process. **EBillZen** simplifies this by providing a digital ecosystem to track usage, calculate bills instantly, and maintain transparency between owners and tenants.

> **Goodbye Khata, Hello System:** এটি একটি অটোমেশন সিস্টেম যা বাড়ির মালিক বা মার্কেট মালিকদের হাতে কলমে হিসাব করার ঝামেলা থেকে মুক্তি দেয়।

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **⚡ Accurate Calculations:** Input meter rates once, enter the current reading, and the system handles the math with zero errors.
* **🤝 Instant Transparency:** Generate digital bill summaries and share them instantly with tenants via **WhatsApp or SMS**.
* **📱 Secure Authentication:** Easy and secure access via **Google Sign-In** or **Phone OTP**.
* **🏢 Multi-Unit Support:** Perfect for residential sub-meters, commercial shop units, or large market complexes.
* **☁️ Cloud Storage:** Your data is stored securely, ensuring you never lose your billing history.
